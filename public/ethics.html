The Eye does not take your photo. It only notices what is visible for a moment, like the colour of your jacket or how you walk past the screen, and turns that into text and an avatar. We do not save your face, we do not keep video, and nothing on this site can be used to identify you in a crowd. Walking through the installation is like passing any public artwork in a hallway: you are seen, but not recorded. If you are reading this because you scanned the QR code, you already chose to look. There is no hidden tracking and no ethical issue beyond that.
